Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about FolioForge portfolio templates and custom build process.

How do I choose between a single-page and multi-page portfolio template?

Single-page templates work well for concise presentations where visitors can scroll through everything quickly. Multi-page templates give you room for detailed case studies and separate sections for about, contact, and archives.

What's the difference between a portfolio template and a portfolio UI kit?

A portfolio template gives you a complete layout with structure, sections, and visual hierarchy already in place. A portfolio UI kit provides reusable components (cards, navbars, hero frames) you can assemble into your own design.

How do I make my portfolio stand out?

Let your work do the heavy lifting. Choose a template that complements your projects without competing with them. Write clear, specific descriptions explaining your thinking and impact, and keep layouts uncluttered.

Can I use a portfolio template to build an actual website?

Yes, absolutely! FolioForge is a custom portfolio building service. You choose the design you love, and our team builds, customizes, and optimizes your live portfolio website with your case studies, projects, and custom domain.
